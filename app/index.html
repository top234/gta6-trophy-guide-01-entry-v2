    ⚠ One trophy can be missed

    Positive Mental Attitude — one meditation spot, at Jason's house, is only available during Chapter 1. There is no mission replay. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    About this guide

    Rarity: no published rarity figures exist yet for GTA VI — the game launched 19 November 2026 and the trackers have not published rarity data. Every card therefore shows its grade only; no percentage has been invented.

    Difficulty and times are the maker's estimates, labelled on each card. Story trophies show “across the story” instead of a separate time, so the time-remaining total is not double-counted. PowerPyx rates the whole set 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours to the Platinum.

    Sources: trophy names and descriptions are the official PlayStation list as published 18 November 2026; guides are written in the maker's own words from PowerPyx, Planet GTA 6, Output Lag and KeenGamer, each cited beside the guide it supports.
